# Khona (খনা)

> Legendary Bengali scholar whose sayings on agriculture, weather, astronomy, and traditional wisdom have been preserved through generations.

## Overview

Khona (খনা) is one of the most celebrated figures in Bengali folklore. Although her historical existence remains uncertain, her sayings—known collectively as **Khanar Bachan (খনার বচন)**—have been passed down orally for centuries and later compiled into various books.

Her observations primarily focus on:

- Agriculture
- Weather forecasting
- Seasonal cycles
- Astronomy
- Traditional astrology
- Rural life
- Folk wisdom

Unlike many classical authors, Khona did **not** leave behind authenticated manuscripts. All surviving works are compilations or later studies based on oral traditions and historical legends.

## Collection

### 01. Khanar Bachan — Ramlal Shil ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Type:** Primary historical compilation

The classic and most authoritative collection of Khona's traditional sayings. This serves as the foundation for understanding Khona's wisdom and is the recommended starting point.

---

### 02. Brihat Khanar Bachan — Sri Suresh Chowdhury ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Type:** Expanded compilation

A comprehensive edition containing a larger collection of sayings, explanatory notes, and additional traditional material gathered from various sources.

---

### 03. Dak O Khanar Bachan ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Type:** Folk wisdom collection

A traditional compilation combining the sayings of **Dak** and **Khona**, two legendary figures associated with Bengali folk wisdom, agriculture, and astrology.

---

### 04. Kingbodonti Khona O Khanar Bachan — Purabi Basu ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

**Type:** Biography & literary study

Explores the legends surrounding Khona, her life, and the cultural significance of her sayings, along with selected interpretations.

---

### 05. Barahamihir O Khona ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

**Type:** Historical & cultural study

Examines the traditional relationship between Khona and the legendary astronomer Varahamihira while discussing the historical and cultural background of the associated legends.

## Historical Notes

Most stories about Khona—including her association with Varahamihira—are regarded as **legendary traditions** rather than established historical facts. Nevertheless, *Khanar Bachan* remains one of the most influential collections of indigenous agricultural and folk knowledge in Bengali literature.
