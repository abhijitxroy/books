# Tarini Khuro Samagra

## Overview

- Author: Satyajit Ray
- First Appearance: Tarini Khuro O Betal (1975)
- Total Stories: 15
- Genre: Adventure Fiction, Fantasy, Supernatural Fiction
- Language: Bengali
- Primary Publication: Sandesh magazine.

Tarini Charan Bandopadhyay, popularly known as Tarini Khuro, is one of Satyajit Ray's most beloved fictional creations. A bachelor in his sixties, Tarini Khuro narrates extraordinary tales from his travels and experiences across India and beyond. His stories seamlessly blend adventure, mystery, humour, fantasy and the supernatural, often leaving readers wondering where reality ends and imagination begins.

---

## Main Characters

- Tarini Charan Bandopadhyay (Tarini Khuro) – The storyteller.
- Nyapla – One of Tarini Khuro's young listeners.
- Chotpoti – An enthusiastic member of the listening group.
- Sunanda – A regular listener of Tarini Khuro's stories.
- Poltu – Another member of the group.
- Bhulu – One of Tarini Khuro's admirers and listeners.

---

## Themes

Tarini Khuro stories are known for their unique blend of:

- Adventure
- Fantasy
- Supernatural Fiction
- Horror
- Humour
- Travel Narratives
- Mystery
- Tall Tales

Unlike Feluda or Professor Shonku, Tarini Khuro stories intentionally blur the line between truth and fiction. Readers are often left to decide whether Tarini Khuro's experiences are believable or products of his remarkable storytelling abilities.

## Quick Facts

- Tarini Khuro is an unmarried Bengali gentleman and an exceptional storyteller.
- He has travelled extensively throughout India during his professional life.
- Most stories are narrated to a group of young listeners in Kolkata.
- The supernatural elements are usually left unexplained, allowing readers to form their own interpretations.
- Tarini Khuro remains one of the finest examples of adventure and fantasy fiction in Bengali literature.
