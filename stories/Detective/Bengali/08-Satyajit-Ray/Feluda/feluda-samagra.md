

# Feluda Samagra

## Overview

- Author: Satyajit Ray
- First Appearance: Feludar Goyendagiri (1965)
- Total Completed Works: 35
- Genre: Detective Fiction, Mystery, Adventure
- Language: Bengali
- Primary Publications: Sandesh, Desh and Anandamela magazines.

Feluda (Pradosh Chandra Mitter) is one of the most celebrated fictional detectives in Bengali literature. Assisted by his cousin Topshe and the writer Lalmohan Ganguly (Jatayu), Feluda solves mysteries across India and abroad using observation, logic and extensive general knowledge.

---

## Main Characters

- Pradosh Chandra Mitter (Feluda) – Detective.
- Tapesh Ranjan Mitter (Topshe) – Cousin and narrator.
- Lalmohan Ganguly (Jatayu) – Writer and Feluda's companion.
- Sidhu Jyatha – Walking encyclopedia and source of information.
- Haripada – Feluda's domestic help.
- Inspector Sarkar – Kolkata Police officer appearing in multiple stories.

## Adaptations

- Sonar Kella (1974) – Directed by Satyajit Ray.
- Joi Baba Felunath (1979) – Directed by Satyajit Ray.
- Multiple television adaptations directed by Sandip Ray.
- Several modern Feluda films have been produced by Sandip Ray based on the original stories.

---

## Quick Facts

- Feluda lives at 21 Rajani Sen Road, Kolkata.
- Feluda is inspired partly by Sherlock Holmes and influenced by Ray's admiration for detective fiction.
- Topshe serves a role similar to Dr. Watson in Sherlock Holmes stories.
- Most stories combine mystery, travel, history, art, archaeology and adventure.
- The series remains one of the finest detective fiction collections in Bengali literature.
