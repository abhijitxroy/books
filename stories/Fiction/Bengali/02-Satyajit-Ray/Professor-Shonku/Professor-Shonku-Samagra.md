# Professor Shonku Samagra

## Overview

- Author: Satyajit Ray
- First Appearance: Byomjatrir Diary (1961)
- Total Completed Stories: 39
- Unfinished Stories: 1 (Drexel Islander Ghatana)
- Genre: Science Fiction, Adventure Fiction
- Language: Bengali
- Primary Publication: Sandesh magazine.

Professor Trilokeshwar Shonku is one of Satyajit Ray's most celebrated fictional characters and a pioneer of Bengali science fiction. A brilliant scientist, inventor and polyglot, Professor Shonku embarks on extraordinary adventures across the world and beyond. His stories seamlessly blend science, mystery, adventure and imagination while exploring themes of artificial intelligence, extraterrestrial life, space exploration and scientific ethics.

The first Professor Shonku story, Byomjatrir Diary (An Astronaut's Diary), was published in Sandesh magazine in 1961. The final adventure, Drexel Islander Ghatana (The Incident of Drexel Island), remained unfinished following the death of Satyajit Ray in 1992.
