# Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the long-term evolution of the **Books** repository as the source library of the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem (PKE).

---

## Vision

Build a research-grade, scalable, searchable, and maintainable source library that preserves books, maps, gazetteers, archives, and reference material for lifelong learning and historical research.

## Relationship with Other Repositories

```text
books (Source Material)
        ↓
india (Research)
        ↓
essentials (Knowledge)
```

The Books repository is the canonical source library for the Personal Knowledge Ecosystem. It stores original source material, bibliographic metadata, per-book summaries, maps, gazetteers, and archival resources. Research outputs, synthesized knowledge, documentary scripts, and learning artifacts belong in downstream repositories such as India and Essentials.
