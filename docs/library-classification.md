# Library Classification Standard

## Purpose

This document defines how books and reference material are organized within the **Books** repository. The objective is to maintain a consistent, scalable, and searchable personal digital library.

---

## Classification Principles

- Every item should have one primary location.
- Classify by subject rather than personal preference.
- Avoid duplicate copies across categories.
- Preserve original source material.
- Keep research, notes, and summaries outside this repository.

---

## Current Top-Level Categories

- Finance
- Growth
- Life
- Mind
- Spiritual
- Stories
- Technology

These categories represent the current organization and will evolve gradually as the library expands.

---

## Future Classification

The long-term taxonomy will introduce additional subject collections such as:

- History
- Biographies
- Politics
- Economics
- Psychology
- Philosophy
- Science
- Geography
- Literature
- Archives

Migration to the expanded taxonomy will occur incrementally rather than through a large-scale reorganization.

---

## Classification Workflow

1. Determine the book's primary subject.
2. Check whether a suitable category already exists.
3. Store the PDF in a single location.
4. Record reading progress separately.
5. Reference the book from other repositories instead of duplicating it.

---

## Status

This standard will evolve as the library grows and new subject areas are introduced.
