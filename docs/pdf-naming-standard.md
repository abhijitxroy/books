# PDF Naming Standard

## Purpose

This document defines the naming convention for PDF files stored in the **Books** repository to ensure consistency, discoverability, and long-term maintainability.

---

## Naming Principles

- Preserve the original filename when it is already clear and meaningful.
- Prefer the official book title over abbreviated names.
- Avoid unnecessary special characters.
- Keep names concise and human-readable.
- Use consistent naming across all collections.

---

## Recommended Format

```text
<Book Title>.pdf
```

Examples:

```text
Atomic Habits.pdf
Clean Architecture.pdf
Echoes from Old Calcutta.pdf
The Psychology of Money.pdf
```

For multi-volume works:

```text
<Book Title> - Volume 1.pdf
<Book Title> - Volume 2.pdf
```

Examples:

```text
Bengal in 1756-1757 - Volume 1.pdf
Feluda Samagra - Volume 3.pdf
```

---

## Avoid

- Random underscores or hyphens.
- Download-site filenames.
- Version numbers unless they are part of the official title.
- Duplicate filenames for different editions.

---

## Future Enhancements

As the library grows, the standard may be extended to include edition, publication year, or language where needed, while keeping filenames simple whenever possible.
