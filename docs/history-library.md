# History Library

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term organization of the history collection within the **Books** repository. The objective is to build a comprehensive, scalable, and research-oriented history library that supports documentary projects and historical research.

---

## Scope

The history library includes:

- World history
- Regional history
- Indian history
- State and city history
- Military history
- Cultural history
- Political history
- Primary source collections
- Gazetteers and archival publications

---

## Proposed Structure

```text
history/
├── world/
├── asia/
├── india/
│   ├── ancient/
│   ├── medieval/
│   ├── modern/
│   ├── freedom-movement/
│   ├── partition/
│   ├── states/
│   │   └── west-bengal/
│   │       ├── kolkata/
│   │       └── districts/
│   └── documentary/
├── primary-sources/
├── maps/
├── gazetteers/
└── archives/
```

---

## Classification Principles

- Organize by geography first, then by historical period where appropriate.
- Keep primary and secondary sources separate.
- Preserve multi-volume works together.
- Avoid duplicate copies.
- Use this structure for all future history acquisitions.

---

## Documentary Support

The history library is intended to support documentary research on:

- India
- West Bengal
- Kolkata
- Historical biographies
- Heritage and architecture
- Culture and society

---

## Status

This structure will evolve as the history collection expands, but changes should prioritize long-term stability and consistency.
