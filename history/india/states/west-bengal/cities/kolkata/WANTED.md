## Workflow

When an item is acquired:

1. Remove it from this list.
2. Add it to the appropriate category under `books/`.
3. Update `MASTER-LIST.md` (if applicable).
4. Update `INVENTORY.md`.
5. Update `ACQUISITION.md`.
