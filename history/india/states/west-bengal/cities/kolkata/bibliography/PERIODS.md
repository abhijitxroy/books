# Kolkata Historical Periods

> Reading guide and classification of books by the historical periods they primarily cover. A single book may appear under multiple periods.

---

## Timeline

| Period | Years | Focus |
|--------|------:|-------|
| Before Kolkata | Before 1690 | Sutanuti, Govindapur, Kalikata villages, regional Bengal |
| Foundation of Kolkata | 1690–1757 | East India Company, early settlement, Fort William |
| Company Rule | 1757–1858 | Plassey, Company administration, growth of Calcutta |
| British Raj | 1858–1905 | Capital of British India, municipal development, society |
| Bengal Renaissance | c.1815–1915 | Education, literature, reform, culture |
| Partition of Bengal | 1905–1911 | Swadeshi movement, politics, nationalism |
| Freedom Movement | 1911–1947 | Independence movement, urban society |
| Partition & Refugees | 1947–1971 | Partition, migration, refugee colonies |
| Modern Kolkata | 1971–Present | Contemporary Kolkata, heritage, urban development |
