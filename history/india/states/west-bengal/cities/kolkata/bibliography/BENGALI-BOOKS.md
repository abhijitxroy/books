# Bengali Books on Kolkata

> This file is a research-oriented, curated reading guide to Bengali-language books about Kolkata. It is derived from the canonical `MASTER-BOOKS.md` bibliography, and is intended to help researchers and enthusiasts navigate the Bengali literature on Kolkata’s history, society, and culture. This is not the canonical bibliography; for authoritative and complete metadata, refer to `MASTER-BOOKS.md`.

## Purpose

- Showcase Bengali-language books only.
- Organize books by subject for focused research.
- Recommend a reading order for structured study.
- Point readers to `MASTER-BOOKS.md` for full bibliographic metadata and verification status.
