- College Street (physical)
- Antiquarian book dealers
- University libraries

## Acquisition Workflow

1. Identify missing title.
2. Search approved sources.
3. Verify edition.
4. Record acquisition source.
5. Download or purchase.
6. Rename according to repository standards.
7. Update MASTER-BOOKS.md and PUBLIC-DOMAIN.md.

## Acquisition Checklist

- [ ] Title identified
- [ ] Source verified
- [ ] Edition verified
- [ ] PDF acquired or physical copy located
- [ ] Metadata added to MASTER-BOOKS.md
- [ ] Verification requested if required
