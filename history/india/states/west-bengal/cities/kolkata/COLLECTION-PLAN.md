# Kolkata Collection Plan

## Purpose

This document defines the roadmap for building the Kolkata History Collection within the **Books** repository. The objective is to create a comprehensive, research-grade collection of authoritative source material for historical study and documentary work.

## Collection Standards

Every item added should:

- Be an authoritative source.
- Prefer complete editions over abridged versions.
- Prefer searchable OCR where available.
- Be recorded in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`.
- Be tracked in `INVENTORY.md`.
- Be documented in `ACQUISITION.md`.
