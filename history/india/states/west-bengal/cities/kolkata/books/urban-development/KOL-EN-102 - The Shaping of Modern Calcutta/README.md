## Why This Book Matters

This book examines the transformation of Calcutta during the Lottery Committee period (1817–1830), documenting the city's early urban planning, civic administration, infrastructure, and institutional development. It is an essential modern reference for understanding how colonial Calcutta evolved into a planned metropolis.
