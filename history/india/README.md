# India History Collection

## Purpose

The **India** collection contains books and historical reference material covering the history of the Indian subcontinent from ancient civilizations to modern India. It serves as the primary source library for research conducted in the `india` repository.

---

## Scope

The collection includes:

- Ancient India
- Medieval India
- Modern India
- Freedom Movement
- Partition of India
- Constitution and Nation Building
- States and Union Territories
- Cities and Local History
- Cultural and Social History
- Political History

## Organization Principles

- Organize by historical period and geography.
- Keep state and city collections under their respective states.
- Preserve multi-volume works together.
- Keep primary sources in the dedicated `history/primary-sources/` collection when appropriate.
