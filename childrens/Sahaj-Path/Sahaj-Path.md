# Sahaj Path

## About

Sahaj Path by Rabindranath Tagore is one of the most beloved Bengali
children's books. It introduces children to the beauty of the Bengali
language through poems, rhythm, nature, and imagination.

## My Favourite Poems

- Talgach
- Hat Bosechhe Bokshiganje
- Amloki Bon
- Khoka Gelo Machh Dhorte

## Why I Keep It

These poems are part of my childhood memories. I hope to read them
again one day with my child and pass on the joy of Bengali literature
to the next generation.
